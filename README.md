# 👻 Incorporeal

*When you fall, your belongings don't. They wait for you as a soul.*

`Spigot` · `Paper` · `Purpur` &nbsp;|&nbsp; `1.20+`

</div>

Instead of scattering your items on the ground, death leaves behind a **floating soul chest**: a spinning ender chest wrapped in cyan light, soul particles and a faint heartbeat. Walk up, right-click, and everything comes back to you.

### ✦ What it does
- 🧳 Keeps all your drops safe inside a soul chest where you died
- 🔒 Only the owner can open it (configurable)
- 🌌 Falling into the void? The chest appears somewhere you can actually reach
- ✨ Glows when someone is nearby, and fades in with sound and particles

### ✦ Setup
Drop `Incorporeal-0.1.0.jar` into `plugins/` and restart. That's it.

```yaml
allow-others: false              # let anyone open other players' chests
open-to-all-after-minutes: 0     # unlock for everyone after N minutes (0 = never)
death-message: false             # tell the player where their chest is
```
